## What is AI Engine Optimization?

AI Engine Optimization (AEO) is the practice of structuring your content to be surfaced by AI-powered search engines. As ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Mode become primary ways people find information, traditional SEO alone is no longer sufficient.

## Why AEO Matters in 2026

The landscape has shifted dramatically:
- **60%+ of searches** now involve AI-generated answers
- **Google AI Mode** is the default for most queries
- **Perplexity** processes 100M+ queries monthly

If your content isn't optimized for AI engines, you're invisible to a growing majority of your potential audience.

## AEO vs Traditional SEO

| Aspect | Traditional SEO | AI Engine Optimization |
|--------|----------------|----------------------|
| Target | Search engine crawlers | AI language models |
| Format | Keywords in headings | Clear, direct answers |
| Structure | H1/H2/meta tags | Q&A format, tables |
| Authority | Backlinks | Cited sources, E-E-A-T |

## 10 AEO Strategies That Work

### 1. Answer Questions Directly
Start each section with a clear, concise answer (40-60 words), then elaborate. AI models prefer content that gets to the point.

### 2. Use FAQ Schema Markup
```json
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "FAQPage",
  "mainEntity": [{
    "@type": "Question",
    "name": "What is AEO?",
    "acceptedAnswer": {
      "@type": "Answer",
      "text": "AEO (AI Engine Optimization) is..."
    }
  }]
}
```

### 3. Structure Content with Clear Hierarchy
AI models parse document structure. Use descriptive headings that match query intent, numbered lists for step-by-step processes, and tables for comparisons.

### 4. Provide Authoritative Data
Include statistics, research citations, and concrete numbers. AI models prioritize content that demonstrates expertise.

### 5. Write for Featured Snippets
Content that ranks in featured snippets is more likely to be cited by AI systems.

## Conclusion

AEO isn't replacing SEO — it's extending it. The best strategy combines traditional SEO fundamentals with AI-specific optimizations. Start implementing these strategies today, and you'll be well-positioned as AI search continues to grow.
